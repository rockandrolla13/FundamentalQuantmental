# Walk-forward 2024 to 2026: the full analyst, quarter by quarter (methodology Section 11)

## Context
The user asked to redo the backtest on seven new issuers from 2024, with predictions on a quarterly basis and a breakdown of each step. The panel backtest (decision page 2026-10-22-alpha-backtest-design) tests one predictor, the fair line, on every TRACE bond each month. It cannot test the four-group analyst: the clocks, cases, gate, skills and books need filings, forecasts and the scenario engine per issuer. So this test runs the whole of `scripts/process.py` at past dates. The panel backtest files are not touched.
Code: `scripts/walkforward.py` (run, audit, outcomes, report), `hy/walkforward.py` (pure functions), `tests/test_walkforward.py`. Data: `data/raw/walkforward/`. Write-up: `docs/writeup/walkforward_2024.tex`.

## Options
1. Extend the panel backtest with more predictors. It covers thousands of bonds, but only predictors that need no filing text, no forecasts and no scenario engine. It does not answer the question asked.
2. Run the full process at each quarter end for a fixed set of issuers, and measure what their bonds then earned (chosen).
3. Run it monthly. It gives three times the dates, but filings change only quarterly, and the runs are slow.

## Choice
Option 2.
- **Issuers.** Seven new, plus the eight of the memos, which sets the cross-section for the fair line at each date. Seven new: HTZ (car rental), CLF (steel), FYBR (telecom), RIG (offshore drilling), CHTR (cable), AAL (airline), OPI (office property trust). Each is an SEC filer with Compustat point-in-time data and secured or senior unsecured bonds trading in TRACE in March 2024 and March 2026. Rejected after the data check: LUMN, BBWI and VSAT (no bonds under the parent ticker in TRACE), SATS (the Compustat ticker maps to another company), CVNA (one public trade in March 2024), UNIT (its SEC registrant has no filings before 2025). Archetypes and their F2 sources are in `data/analyst_inputs/<ticker>.yaml`.
- **Dates.** Each quarter end t from 2024-03-31 where t plus 3 months is on or before the last TRACE date (2026-09-17). That gives nine dates, 2024-03-31 to 2026-03-31. Six-month results exist for the first eight.
- **Point in time.** The process runs as known at t + 1 day:
  - filings accepted on or before then (`clock_inputs.latest_filings`);
  - Compustat quarters with `available_at` on or before then; a blank cash-flow quarter is filled only from a filing accepted by then (`xbrl.cash_flow_from_filing`);
  - TRACE trades through t, with a one-day lag;
  - the Treasury curve on or before t;
  - forecast rows with `available_at` on or before then (`forecast_sources.load_all`).
  
  The fair line is fitted on that date's cross-section. `scripts/walkforward.py audit` re-checks every run. It checks the decision date, each forecast source's last vintage, the curve date, and each issuer's filing acceptance against the EDGAR index. A breach raises `LookAheadError`.
- **Weights and size.** The user's five buckets (decision page 2026-10-22-four-group-combination) and S14b as built. Both were set in October 2026, so they are not point in time. The S14b slope comes from a backtest whose sample overlaps this window. The write-up says so.
- **Outcome.** The issuer's bonds that the model priced at t are each held to t + h (h = 3, 6 months). The exit is the last TRACE quote in (t, t + h]; a bond that stops trading exits at its last price. Per bond (`hy.walkforward.bond_realised`), the result is the clean price change plus coupon, less a duration-matched Treasury, less beta x the high-yield benchmark's spread return. Beta is the model's DTS hedge, s_bond / S_index; beta = 1 is stored beside it. The model's round-trip cost (20 bp of spread x duration) is subtracted. The issuer's result is the mean over its bonds, annualised to bp a year.
- **Scores.** Per predictor:
  - mean quarterly rank IC across issuers (5 or more issuers per date) and its t-statistic across quarters;
  - share of quarters with a positive IC;
  - pooled rank IC;
  - hit rate;
  - slope of realised on predicted, with standard errors clustered by quarter.
  
  After-cost predictions meet after-cost results; buckets and estimates (before cost) meet before-cost results.
- **Events.** Timelines mark credit events from 8-K item codes (1.03 bankruptcy, 2.04 acceleration, 3.01 delisting notice, 2.01 deal completed, 5.01 change in control, 1.01 with 2.03 new debt agreement) and the dates results were filed. They are for display only, never an input. No rating history exists in the data, so downgrades are not marked.

## Test
- `tests/test_walkforward.py`: decision dates (the last date has three months of TRACE after it); decision time t + 1 day; the look-ahead guard refuses a late input; the exit quote reads only (t, t + h]; the realised return by hand; no accrual when trading flat; scores on a perfect and a reversed predictor; thin dates skipped; gate outcomes; 8-K labels.
- What the result can and cannot settle: with about 15 issuers and 9 quarters, a mean IC must exceed about 0.2 to stand out from noise. This test can catch a predictor that ranks the wrong way. It cannot calibrate weights. The weights decision (four-group page, test 0) needs the panel-scale backtest with the clocks built point in time.
