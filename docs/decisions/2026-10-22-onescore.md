# OneScore: equity momentum (EMC) combined with ESP

Date: 22 October 2026. Code: `hy/peers.py` (`equity_momentum`, `onescore`), `hy/sources/peer_data.py` (`equity_monthly`), `scripts/backtest_onescore.py`.

## Context
ESP adds to spread level over the full sample but fails the three-of-four-cycles bar beyond spread (`2026-10-22-esp-peer-groups.md`). Dynkin et al. (2021, ch. 18 and 19) combine ESP with equity momentum in credit (EMC) into OneScore. In their investment grade data, ESP is pro-cyclical and EMC is contra-cyclical, so the mix is steadier in drawdowns.

## What the source says
1. EMC = the issuer's stock return over a formation period. The main case uses 3 months (ch. 15). The signal decays fast.
2. OneScore = issuer-level ESP and EMC, each normalised within the industry sector, then combined (ch. 19). Chapter 18 combines the two with equal weights.
3. The method table of ch. 19 did not survive the PDF conversion. The build below follows the text.

## What we built
1. Stock returns: `comp.secm` monthly total return (`trt1m`) of the primary US issue. CRSP on this licence ends in December 2025.
2. EMC = compounded return over the 3 month ends to t, known at t + 1 day.
3. Issuer ESP = median `esp_sr` over the issuer's bonds. Each part becomes a percentile rank within its GICS sector (5 issuers minimum). OneScore = the mean of the two ranks.
4. Only listed issuers have EMC: 52% of bond-months from 2016 on, fewer before.

## Test result (2008 to 2026, listed issuers only, one common sample)

| Signal | Beyond spread 3m (t) | 6m (t) | 12m (t) | Cycles positive beyond spread (6m) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ESP, sector x rating | +0.025 (1.6) | +0.023 (1.0) | +0.029 (0.9) | 1 of 4 |
| EMC, 3-month stock return | +0.038 (4.8) | +0.027 (2.6) | +0.024 (2.3) | 3 of 4 |
| OneScore | +0.039 (3.3) | +0.034 (2.1) | +0.037 (1.7) | 3 of 4 |

Raw IC after cost at 6 months: widest 0.336, ESP 0.231, OneScore 0.165, EMC 0.023. The ESP and EMC ranks are negatively correlated (-0.11 on average), in every cycle window.

## What the data says
1. EMC adds to spread level, and it works in stress: beyond-spread IC +0.10 in the 2008 window, where ESP is -0.07.
2. OneScore is the first peer signal to pass the three-of-four-cycles bar beyond spread. The pass is narrow: +0.01 in 2008 and +0.02 in 2015. Both parts fail in the 2020 window.
3. Among listed issuers, ESP alone adds little beyond spread (t 1.0 at 6 months). Its full-sample edge (t 2.6) comes partly from private issuers, which OneScore cannot score.
4. EMC decays with horizon, as the book says. Its edge is strongest at 3 months.

## Decision
1. Report OneScore, EMC and the two sector ranks in every memo (step 9).
2. User decision, same day: split E14's 9% with OneScore. E14 ESP 4.5%, E16 OneScore 4.5%. An unlisted issuer has no OneScore, so `scripts/process.py` sets its E16 to its E14 and ESP carries the full 9%.
