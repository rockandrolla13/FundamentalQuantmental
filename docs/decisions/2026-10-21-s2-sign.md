# S2 fundamental momentum: sign of the formula

## Context
Section 8, S2, stated `S2 = Δlog ŝ − Δlog s` (fair change minus market change). The text below the formula says that a positive value means that fundamentals improved more than the spread tightened. When fundamentals improve, `ŝ` falls, so the old formula gave a negative value. The formula and the text disagreed.

## Options
1. Keep the formula. Change the text: negative means long.
2. Keep the text. Flip the formula: `S2 = Δlog s − Δlog ŝ`.

## Choice
Option 2. Section 8 states that every signal is positive for a long. The text and the signal table agree with that rule. Only the formula did not.

## Test
`tests/test_signals.py::test_s2_positive_when_fundamentals_lead_spread`: the fair spread falls by 20% (0.050 to 0.040), the market spread falls by 5% (0.050 to 0.0475), and S2 must be positive. The test passes.
