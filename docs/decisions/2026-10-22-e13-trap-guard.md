# E13 trap guard: the three warning signs of Kricheff p. 124

Date: 22 October 2026. Code: `hy/books.py` (`spt_trap_guard`, `SPT_TRAP`), `scripts/process.py` (`group_estimates`). Tests: `tests/test_four_groups.py` (`test_trap_guard_*`). Card: `research/cards/kricheff-pragmatists-guide-leveraged-finance.md`.

## Context
E13 scores spread per turn of leverage (SPT). A trap guard sets E13 to 0 for a bond whose wide SPT hides a credit problem. The old guard set E13 to 0 unless FCF / debt > 0 and TEV / debt > 1. The Kricheff card shows that this guard passes the book's own trap bond.

## What the source says
1. The DataFone 9% Senior Notes have the widest SPT in the book's table (PDF p. 124).
2. The book calls them risky for three reasons. Total leverage is 7x, the highest in the table. FCF / debt is 0.1%, "meaningfully lower" than every comparable. The equity value implies "a minimal asset value cushion".
3. The book gives no numeric cut-offs. "Highest" and "lower than any comparable" are peer-relative words.

## What the data says
1. DataFone has FCF / debt > 0 and a cushion above 0, so the old guard gave it full E13 credit.
2. On the 8-issuer run of 22 October 2026, the old and new guards agree. Three issuers (CRWV, ORCL, NCLH) fail the hard floor on negative FCF. No issuer has two soft signs and a passing hard floor.
3. In that run, three of eight issuers burn cash. The peer 20% quantile of FCF / debt is −17%. A pure peer cut-off would miss a DataFone at +0.1%.

## Options
1. Keep the old guard. It misses the book's own case.
2. Fixed cut-offs only. Simple, but the book's signs are peer-relative.
3. Peer cut-offs only. A run of cash burners drags the FCF cut below zero (data point 3).
4. Peer cut-offs joined with fixed cut-offs, two of three signs for a trap. Chosen.

## What we decide
1. Keep the hard floor: FCF / debt <= 0 or TEV / debt <= 1, or either unknown, is a trap.
2. Sign 1, high leverage: total leverage >= the lower of the run's 80th percentile and 6.0x.
3. Sign 2, low FCF: FCF / debt <= the higher of the run's 20th percentile and 2%.
4. Sign 3, thin cushion: TEV / debt <= 1.25, that is equity below 20% of debt.
5. Two or more signs is a trap. One sign alone is the risk that SPT pays for.
6. A run with fewer than 5 peer values uses only the fixed cut-offs.

The cut-offs 80%, 20%, 6.0x, 2% and 1.25 are our choice. The book does not set them.

## Test that settles it
On the TRACE high yield panel (`scripts/backtest_peers.py`), compute the beyond-spread rank IC of E13 at 6 and 12 months under three rules: no guard, the old guard, and this guard. Keep this guard only if it beats both in at least three of the four cycle windows (2008, 2015, 2020, 2022). Second test: split the top SPT decile each month by the count of signs. The book predicts that bonds with two or more signs underperform the rest of the decile. The backtest panel does not yet carry FCF / debt and TEV / debt per issuer-month, so both tests are open.
