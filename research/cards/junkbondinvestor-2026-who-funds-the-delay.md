# JunkBondInvestor (2026), "Credit Weekly: Who Funds the Delay?"

Newsletter post, Substack, 8 November 2026. Read by the user in email on 9 November 2026. Tier: practitioner commentary, the lowest tier of the research protocol. The facts below are the author's claims. We did not check them.

## Claim
A delay in a data centre build opens a funding gap before any contract fails. The coupons fall due on fixed dates, and the rent arrives later. The sponsor often fills the gap. Many sponsors are former bitcoin miners that fund with stock and convertible bonds, and the November 2026 vote cut those stock prices.

## Method
Worked example and reasoning. $2bn of five-year secured notes at 10%. Rent starts in 18 months. A $300mm interest reserve carries the coupons to then. A one-year delay empties the reserve one year before the first rent and opens $200mm of extra interest.

## Data
None tested. Market facts the author states: Cipher Digital stock fell 32% from Tuesday to Thursday close after the vote. It fell about 13% at the announcement of an $850mm zero-coupon convertible and 12% at pricing. Developer stocks fell 20% to 50% over one month. Voters sided against data centres in 40 of 41 local measures.

## Result
Five items to read per issuer:
1. Months of slip that the interest reserve covers.
2. Completion support: cash set aside, or a promise to fund later. Capped or uncapped.
3. Whether the sponsor can fund a gap without new stock.
4. What the tenant owes while it waits: rent credits, early rent, an outside date to walk away, force majeure.
5. Whether taxes and extra costs pass through to the tenant.

Energized capacity gains replacement value. For a bondholder, that gain shows in re-lease and refinance value, not in today's coupons.
